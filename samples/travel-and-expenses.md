# Travel and expenses policy

Baobab Logistics Ltd is a fictional company. This document exists only to demonstrate the application.

## Booking travel

Staff travelling to Mombasa, Kisumu or Eldoret for work may book economy flights or standard-class SGR train tickets. Business-class travel is not reimbursed. Bookings are made through the finance office at least five working days before departure.

## Accommodation

Hotel stays are capped at KES 12,000 per night in Mombasa and KES 9,000 per night elsewhere in Kenya. Staff should share transport from the airport where possible.

## Daily allowance

A daily allowance of KES 3,500 covers meals and local transport on travel days. No allowance is paid when meals are provided by a client or event organiser.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal with itemised receipts within 30 days of returning. Claims submitted later are paid only with approval from the finance director. M-Pesa payment confirmations count as receipts.
