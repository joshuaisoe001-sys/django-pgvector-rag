# IT security policy

Baobab Logistics Ltd is a fictional company. This document exists only to demonstrate the application.

## Laptops

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes without use. Personal software may be installed only with written approval from the IT team.

## Passwords and accounts

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the HR portal and the finance portal. Passwords must never be shared with anyone, including the IT helpdesk, which will never ask for them.

## Incidents

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT security team within 24 hours, by phone or at security@baobab.example. Suspicious emails should be forwarded to the same address and then deleted.

## Data

Customer delivery records may only be stored on company systems. Copying them to USB drives or personal cloud storage is not allowed.
