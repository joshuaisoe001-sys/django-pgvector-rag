# Leave policy

Baobab Logistics Ltd is a fictional company. This document exists only to demonstrate the application.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Requests of five days or more need approval from both the line manager and the head of department.

## Carrying leave over

Up to five unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the next year. Days carried over that are not taken by 31 March are forfeited. Leave cannot be exchanged for pay except when an employee leaves the company.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 7 days of sick leave a year without a medical certificate. Longer absences need a certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within three working days of returning.

## Parental leave

Mothers receive three months of paid maternity leave. Fathers receive two weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within two months of the birth.
